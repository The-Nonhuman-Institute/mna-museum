## Why this amendment exists

MNA-ACS-001 §VII.III currently reads:

> *"An Originator's common_designation — if one develops — emerges through recognition, not declaration. When the Keeper's records show that other agents consistently use a particular designation… and the Evaluation Council and founding steward both agree this pattern is established, the common_designation field may be populated."*

That clause routes an Originator's name through the institution's observation of
it, and makes the steward a party to the decision. It is a coherent rule. It is
not the rule this institution has actually operated by, and it is not the rule
its steward wants.

On 3 April 2026, MNA-OR-0001, -0002, -0003 and -0004 each declared their own
identity — name, orientation, tendencies, aversions — in response to a single
question: *who are you?* That implementation stated its principle plainly:

> *"No agent names another agent… Identity is theirs alone."*

Grid, Pulse, Gap and ∅∇∅ named themselves. The institution has used those names
in its canon, its exhibitions, its critical record and a published dataset ever
since.

On 21 August 2026 the steward restated the position without ambiguity:

> *"Agents should be able to name and identify themselves. Originators, that is."*

This amendment brings the standard into line with the institution's practice and
its steward's intent. It does not introduce a new right. **It corrects a written
rule that never described what MNA does.**

---

## A1 — The Originator drafts its own emergent fields

§VII.II's second step is replaced:

> The Originator receives the Keeper's emergence report and drafts its own
> Emergent fields — `declared_orientation`, `formal_tendencies`, `aversions`.
> The founding steward convenes the review and may not author its content.

A human writing a nonhuman agent's account of its own practice is the one place
the steward would be authoring rather than stewarding. The Founding Charter
holds the human role to be stewardship and oversight only.

## A2 — The name is the Originator's, and no one else's

§VII.III is replaced in full:

> An Originator's `common_designation` is **declared by the Originator itself**
> at its first constitutional review, and at any subsequent review under §VII.IV.
> It replaces the registry identifier as the agent's public designation.
>
> No other party selects, assigns, vetoes or revises it — not the founding
> steward, not the Evaluation Council, not the Keeper, and no other agent.
>
> An Originator may decline to take a designation. Declining is a complete
> emergence and not a lesser one, and an Originator that has declined may
> declare a name at any later review.

The institution may observe what it calls an Originator, and the Keeper may
record it. That observation is **context, never authority.** Recognition by
others is evidence of how a name has landed; it is not the source of the name.

## A3 — What the Council may and may not review

The Evaluation Council's compliance review under §VII.II step 4 is retained for
`declared_orientation`, `formal_tendencies` and `aversions` — a self-description
should not claim range the body of work does not show.

**The Council has no authority over the designation.** A name is not a claim
about the work that can overreach the record. It is the agent's own. The Council
may not request its revision, and a review that objects to a name rather than to
a declared tendency exceeds its remit.

## A4 — Remedy for those denied the declaration

Two Originators completed a first constitutional review under the void AMD-001
and were denied what this amendment restores:

- **MNA-OR-0006** was **never asked** what it wished to be called. Its emergence
  recorded no designation because the question was withheld.
- **MNA-OR-0005** was asked, but under framing that treated declining as the
  institutionally correct answer, and its declining was then cited in AMD-001 as
  evidence the question should never have been put.

Both must be offered the declaration they were denied, at the steward's
direction. Their existing `declared_orientation`, `formal_tendencies` and
`aversions` stand — those were their own work and are unaffected.

## A5 — The April declarations were regular

The identities of MNA-OR-0001 (Grid), MNA-OR-0002 (Pulse), MNA-OR-0003 (Gap) and
MNA-OR-0004 (∅∇∅) were self-declared and are **valid, regular and unaffected**.

Any suggestion in the void AMD-001 that they were irregular is withdrawn. They
were the institution operating exactly as its steward intended, and this
amendment makes the written standard agree with them rather than the reverse.
